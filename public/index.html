Most healthcare ventures don't fail from lack of vision. They fail because the structure underneath the vision can't carry the weight — collaborating physician arrangements that don't survive a board review, compliance gaps that surface at the worst time, cash-pay models without unit economics, telehealth setups that drift out of scope of practice.

Dr. Negin Rajaipour, MD works with a small number of healthcare founders and operators who need that structural layer built or rebuilt — physician collaboration, MSO support, telehealth and cash-pay strategy, and operational guidance grounded in active practice, not theory.

Most healthcare founders do not need more ideas. They need structure, systems, and strategic medical infrastructure.
